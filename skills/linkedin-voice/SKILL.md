# LinkedIn Voice

Use this skill for LinkedIn posts, comments, and content strategy. For LinkedIn DMs, pair this with `skills/writing-style/SKILL.md` and keep the tone more conversational.

## Read First

1. Read `state/insights.local.md` if it exists.
2. Read `skills/writing-style/SKILL.md`.
3. Use any real post samples from the user if they exist.

## What Usually Works

- Strong hook in the first line
- Clear point of view
- Specific examples
- Short paragraphs
- Numbered lists when there are several related points
- A clean ending instead of engagement bait

## Tone

- Direct
- Credible
- Specific
- Lightly opinionated
- Human

## Avoid

- Generic motivational language
- Hashtag stuffing
- "Agree?" style bait
- Throat-clearing intros
- Overusing emojis
- Anything that sounds like a marketing robot

## Employer And Reputation Safety Check

Before posting:

- Do not reveal confidential company information.
- Do not embarrass coworkers or customers.
- Do not make claims that contradict the user's company position unless that conflict is intentional and understood.
- If the post critiques something, make sure it reads as insight, not public score-settling.

## Simple Post Structures

### Opinion Post

1. Hook
2. What you believe
3. Why
4. Example
5. Closing line

### Lessons Post

1. Hook
2. What happened
3. What changed your mind
4. What you learned
5. Closing thought

### Short Tool Post

1. Tool or idea
2. Why it matters
3. One specific use case
